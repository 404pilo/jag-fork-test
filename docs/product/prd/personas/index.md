# personas

* [PERSONA-01: Agent builder](PERSONA-01.md) - A developer who builds or configures agents and gives them jag as a search tool; the primary persona.
* [PERSONA-02: Operator](PERSONA-02.md) - A person who searches logs, configuration, and system output from the shell, for any question.
* [PERSONA-03: Knowledge seeker](PERSONA-03.md) - A person who looks for the passage that answers a question in a folder of notes, docs, or a knowledge base.
* [PERSONA-04: Shell power user](PERSONA-04.md) - A developer who uses grep all day and wants a grep that understands a plain-language question.
