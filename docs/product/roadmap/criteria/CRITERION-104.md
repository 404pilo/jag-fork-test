# CRITERION-104: The size-limit notice is named fields in structured output

In structured output, when jag leaves out results or cuts the top result, the fields left_out, top_result_cut, size_limit_fix, and warnings_left_out give the counts and the fix text of the size-limit notice, and the error stream holds no size-limit notice.
